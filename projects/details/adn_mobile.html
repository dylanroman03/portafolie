ADN Mobile - App de Ventas

Flutter Dart SQLite

ADN Mobile is an application developed in Flutter, designed to optimize field sales operations, seamlessly integrating with ADN Software’s enterprise management and accounting systems. Currently, in production, the app boasts over 1,000 active users and is utilized by various companies, offering a robust and versatile solution for sales and logistics management, both online and offline.


Key Features:
  • Invoice Printing: Generate and print invoices directly from mobile devices using Bluetooth printers.
  • Tracking and Visits: Real-time location logging to document client visits, enhancing traceability.
  • Inventory Management: Efficient control of products and categories with stock management and advanced filters for clients and products.
  • Sales Modalities: Support for self-sale, pre-sale, and cart management, allowing users to save purchases for later processing.
  • Transport Dispatch: Streamlines the management of delivery guides for logistics operations.
  • Real-Time Synchronization: Automatic data updates with ADN Software’s central system.
  • Multi-Account Support: Enables management of multiple user accounts.
  • Collections Management: Tracks collections with the option to block users due to outstanding debts.

Built with a focus on scalability and user experience, ADN Mobile has transformed how businesses manage their sales and distribution processes, delivering a comprehensive solution that combines mobility, efficiency, and connectivity.
