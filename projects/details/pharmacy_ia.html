Pharmacy AI Chatbot

On Production
Node.js Javascript SQL MariaDB IA OpenAI

The Pharmacy AI Chatbot is an innovative, AI-powered solution designed to enhance customer service for pharmacies. Integrated with the pharmacy’s database, the chatbot interacts with customers via text, responding to inquiries about product availability and prices without revealing its AI nature. It intelligently handles vague or unclear requests by leveraging natural language processing to recommend relevant products based on available stock or inform customers when no suitable items are available.


Key Features:

  • Real-Time Inventory Search: Queries the pharmacy’s database to check product availability and provide accurate pricing.
  • Intelligent Recommendations: Suggests alternative products based on stock when customers provide vague or incomplete product names.
  • Seamless Customer Interaction: Engages users naturally, maintaining a conversational tone to ensure a user-friendly experience.
  • Out-of-Stock Handling: Informs customers when no suitable products are available, improving transparency.

Built with a focus on enhancing customer experience and operational efficiency, the Pharmacy AI Chatbot streamlines product inquiries, reduces manual workload for staff, and delivers intelligent, stock-aware responses to drive customer satisfaction.
